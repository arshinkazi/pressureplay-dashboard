How the rankings were built

Pressure is a match state, not simply the final overs. The analysis labels pressure before each delivery, using the chase rate, current rate, wickets in hand, balls remaining and innings stage. Batting and bowling have separate rules.

Batting measures

Pressure strike rate and average, strike-rate uplift against the player's non-pressure baseline, and the share of pressure innings at or above the player's own baseline strike rate.

Bowling measures

Pressure economy and bowling strike rate, economy improvement against the player's non-pressure baseline, and the share of pressure innings at or below the player's own baseline economy.

Scoring

  • Components are winsorised at the 2nd and 98th percentiles and standardised within format-season groups.
  • Batting weights: 35% pressure strike rate, 25% pressure average, 20% uplift and 20% consistency.
  • Bowling weights: 35% pressure economy, 25% bowling strike rate, 20% economy improvement and 20% consistency.
  • A sample-size adjustment shrinks scores with fewer pressure balls.
  • Final order is the average rank across core and relaxed pressure definitions.

Scope and limits

IPL and T20 Internationals, 2022 to 1 November 2026. Eligibility requires at least 100 pressure balls and eight pressure innings. Pressure is a proxy based on match state, not a direct measure of psychology. This dashboard does not include auction registration, price, fitness or role data.

The rankings are descriptive scouting evidence. Combine them with role, availability, fitness and auction-price checks before a recruitment decision.
